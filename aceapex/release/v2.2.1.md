# ACEAPEX 2.2.1 — the encoder keeps to its thread budget

Bug-fix release. Archive bytes are identical to 2.2.0 (54 of 54 combinations of silesia, enwik8, chr1 x
levels 1-3 x 1/8 threads x default/open/interactive profiles; fixtures unchanged). Format ACEPX2 unchanged.

## Fixed
- **The encoder started threads it was not given.** With `threads=1`, 2.2.0 still compressed its three
  token streams on three threads and its literal lanes on up to one thread per CPU. A single-thread
  benchmark therefore measured more than one core: lzbench `-I1` showed 146 % CPU, and level 3 on
  silesia.tar reported 304 MB/s where one core does 195 MB/s. 2.2.1 runs the LZ workers, the literal lanes
  and the token streams inside the call's budget, with the calling thread as one of the workers:
  `threads=1` starts no thread. A new judge claim (`head_enc_threads`) counts the threads the library
  starts at `threads=1` - compress, decompress and region, four profiles, DNA and text - and requires zero.

## Corrected numbers
The single-thread compression figures published with 2.2.0 for lzbench overstated one core. silesia.tar,
EPYC 4344P, pinned to one core (`taskset -c 0`), lzbench `-I1`:

| level | compress MB/s | decompress MB/s | ratio % |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 86.1 | 1107 | 32.56 |
| 2 | 66.2 | 1121 | 32.38 |
| 3 | 195 | 1036 | 31.44 |

Multi-threaded encoding is unchanged (silesia -2 with 8 threads: 0.77 s against 0.78 s). Decompression was
not affected.
