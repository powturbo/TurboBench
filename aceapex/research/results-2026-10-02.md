## I2. Self-repair of bit flips

The 16 MiB chr1 slice of T-H4 in the open profile (4 469 784 B, md5 84eba878...). `research/selfheal.cpp`: the decoder
names the failing unit (literal chunk, token chunk, block, layout), every bit of its bytes is flipped in turn, a probe
re-decodes only what the bit touches, XXH3 of the output is the oracle. found = the repaired archive == the original
bytes; no locator = every unit decodes and only the oracle fails (bytes stored raw). 16 copies at a time.

| flips | copies | found | not found | no locator | false | harmless | probes per copy (mean) | CPU per copy, s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1000 | 990 | 0 | 10 | 0 | 0 | 72 142 | 1.331 |
| 2 | 100 | 93 | 7 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 148 579 | 2.624 |

With two flips a probe that makes its unit and that unit's blocks decode is kept and the next failing unit is searched
(the oracle runs at the end); the 7 not found: both flips in one unit or the first fix not the original bit.

## I2 (step 2). Block hashes as a sidecar, three flips, the GPU path

Same archive as I2 (64 KiB token chunks, md5 84eba878...); sidecar = XXH3_64 of every block of the intact output, 8 B x
1024 blocks = 8 192 B (not in the format). With it a block must also match its hash, and a probe is judged on the blocks
it touches. GPU path (`SH_GPU=1`): literal-chunk probes through the GPU library's plan check and job steps (axo_parse,
rANS pieces, k_open_cg steps, bases), batched 4096 per launch; the H100 time is estimated from the measured H100 rate of
open chunks (t2t.open library decode: 47 212 open chunks in 19.06 ms, 946591a) for whole batches.

| run | copies | found | not found | no locator | false | probes per copy | CPU per copy, s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 flip, no sidecar (I2) | 1000 | 990 | 0 | 10 | 0 | 72 142 | 1.331 |
| 1 flip, sidecar | 1000 | 1000 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 73 018 | 1.325 |
| 3 flips, no sidecar | 100 | 84 | 16 | 0 | 0 | 223 274 | 3.714 |
| 3 flips, sidecar | 100 | 94 | 6 | 0 | 0 | 222 713 | 3.832 |
| 1 flip, sidecar, GPU path | 1000 | 1000 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 73 018 (69 896 219 literal-chunk probes in all) | 1.414 (CPU emulation) |

GPU path: 17 546 launches of 4096 probes = 17.5 per copy; H100 estimate 29.0 ms per copy (launches x 4096 probes at
2.48 M open chunks per second). Probes per copy are not fewer with the sidecar: the 10 copies without a locator are now
searched too; the oracle per probe is the touched blocks' hashes instead of the 16 MiB output.
